Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Who we are

Tir Chonaill Careers is a recruitment business based in Northern Ireland. We handle personal information submitted through our website in connection with recruitment services.

For privacy enquiries, contact info@tir-chonaill-careers.com .

2. Information we collect

When you apply for a vacancy or upload your CV, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address and telephone number.
  • Your CV, employment history, qualifications and experience.
  • The vacancy you are interested in.
  • Any additional information you choose to provide.
  • The date and time you submit your application.

Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in your CV or message.

3. How we use your information

We use candidate information to:

  • Review applications and assess suitability for vacancies.
  • Contact candidates about recruitment opportunities.
  • Manage recruitment enquiries and applications.
  • Maintain appropriate records of recruitment activity.

We do not sell candidate personal information.

4. Our lawful basis

We process recruitment information where necessary to take steps at your request before entering into an employment-related arrangement, and/or where we have a legitimate interest in providing recruitment services and assessing candidates.

Where we rely on consent for a particular use of your information, you may withdraw that consent by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

5. Who can access your information

Candidate applications are accessible only to authorised recruitment personnel and service providers who need access for legitimate recruitment or technical purposes.

Where appropriate, relevant candidate information may be shared with prospective employers in connection with suitable recruitment opportunities. We will explain such sharing to candidates as appropriate.

6. How we store and protect information

Applications are stored using Supabase, a cloud-based service. CV files are held in private storage rather than being made publicly available on our website.

We use access controls and other technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. How long we keep your information

We normally retain candidate applications and CVs for up to 12 months from submission so that we can consider candidates for relevant recruitment opportunities.

Information may be retained for longer where necessary to comply with a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We will review information and securely delete it when it is no longer required.

You may request deletion earlier by emailing info@tir-chonaill-careers.com , subject to any applicable legal exceptions.

8. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, objection to processing, or data portability.

To exercise your rights, contact info@tir-chonaill-careers.com .

You also have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) , the UK's data protection regulator.

9. International processing

Some technology providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where international transfers occur, we aim to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our recruitment practices, technology or legal obligations change. The latest version will be available on this page.
